About Dossier

Make the work visible.

Dossier is a quiet, practical workspace for people who want their resume to sound like them and still meet the moment.

Built for the revision.

The strongest applications are rarely written in one sitting. Dossier keeps your versions close, makes changes easy to see, and gives you a useful signal when a job description starts to drift away from your experience.

Clarity over tricks.

Our scanner finds meaningful overlap without pretending a percentage can replace judgment. Use the result as a checklist, then write the honest version only you can write.
